# Begin where your ambiguity lives

The projects are not one mandatory dependency chain. These paths organize the public material by
the outcome a reader is trying to reach.

## [Understand](/journeys/understand/)

Ground agentic system choices in evidence-backed principles.

## [Plan work](/journeys/plan-work/)

Install focused agent guidance, then express governed engineering work through AEP.

## [Specify](/journeys/specify/)

Turn system intent into executable specifications, contracts, and conformance evidence with ESS.

## [Build agents](/journeys/build-agents/)

Run a provider-neutral agent loop with Harness and observe or compare it with Metaharness.

## [Operate services](/journeys/operate-services/)

Expose governed contracts through AEP Service and supporting product-neutral services.
