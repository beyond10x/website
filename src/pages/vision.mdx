# Make agentic work explicit enough to trust

beyond10x is building an open, product-neutral stack for work that agents can plan, execute, and
prove without hiding authority or evidence inside a prompt transcript.

The stack begins with **Agentic Principles** grounded in observed work. **Entity Runtime** turns
state, rules, operations, and events into deterministic decisions. **AEP** governs engineering
work, while **ESS** makes system intent executable and testable. **Harness** runs model and tool
loops; **Metaharness** observes and compares them. **Substrate**, services, and connectors bound the
effects that reach the outside world.

The reading order is conceptual, not a claim that every project depends on the next. Every
technical detail remains owned by its source repository and is displayed here at an immutable
revision.

## What should be different

- Decisions, permissions, evidence, and failure are data rather than implicit convention.
- Deterministic mechanisms carry guarantees that do not belong in model behavior.
- External effects are attributable, bounded, inspectable, and recoverable.
- Public documentation tells readers what exists, what is experimental, and what remains planned.
